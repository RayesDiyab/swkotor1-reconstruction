# Project status

Current work focuses on the remaining minigames. These are unfinished work, not intentional omissions.

Reported remaining functions include unused Neverwinter multiplayer remnants, debug tools, and cheats. Their classifications still need verification; inclusion or intentional omission has not been settled for every function.

Compatibility testing is ongoing. Full gameplay compatibility remains unconfirmed.

Planned documentation will cover reconstruction provenance, compatibility results and limitations, and intentional omissions with their rationale.
